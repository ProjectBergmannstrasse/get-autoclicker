What it does

  • Auto-clicks at a rate, button and pattern you set
  • Records your real mouse and keyboard, then replays it at 0.5x, 1x or 2x
  • Panic stop: shove the pointer into the top-left corner and everything halts at once
  • F8 starts and stops, F9 pauses and resumes, Esc three times quits
  • Installs its own four dependencies on first run, after asking - nothing to set up by hand
  • One file per system: Windows, macOS, and a universal build for Linux or anything else
  • Works fully offline once installed; the progress and permission screens are built from the standard library alone
  • Handles non-US keyboards (AltGr) and can open the right macOS permission panes for you
